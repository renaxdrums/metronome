# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Musicians using the metronome and tuner as everyday practice tools.

## Product Purpose
RENAX DRUMS provides a metronome with rhythm, training, sequencing and track workspaces, and a separate chromatic tuner. The home surface gives direct access to both.

## Operating Context
The tools run in a browser, including desktop and mobile layouts. The metronome has six workspaces. The tuner uses the device microphone when activated.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The existing deep links `?tool=metronome` and `?tool=tuner` must remain usable. Four approved themes are dark, light, ivory and avant. The metronome and tuner keep their current behavior and settings.

## Brand Commitments
The RENAX DRUMS name and approved fox logo are established assets. The existing orange accent is a recognizable brand element.

## Evidence on Hand
Current `index.html`, `musician-tools.css`, the approved logo in `assets/renax-logo-approved.svg`, and navigation tests in `tests/tuner-interface-test.cjs`.

## Product Principles
- Reach either tool immediately.
- Preserve working musical state across navigation.
- Make frequent use fast and predictable.
